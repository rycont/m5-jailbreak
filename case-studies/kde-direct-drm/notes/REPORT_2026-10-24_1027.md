## Fix chain

1. Debian debuginfod build-id `ba4770b6b33dfbf42b13a5fc21dd75eefe44a377`
   fetched as `/tmp/libkwin.debug` (74,436,048 bytes).
2. First crash LR offset `0x58d7d0` mapped to
   `PointerSwipeGestureV1Interface::sendCancel()` / `qmap.h:1120`.
   Patched `0x58d6bc` → `bx lr`.
3. Next crash `AbstractEglBackend::initClientExtensions() +84`, source
   `abstract_egl_backend.cpp:199`. Patched `0x50d77c` → `bx lr`.
4. KWin then stayed alive and exposed `/run/wayland-4`.
5. Plasma initially failed with `DBus session bus not found`; starting Tizen
   `/usr/bin/dbus-daemon` with `env -u LD_LIBRARY_PATH` fixed that.
6. Plasma then failed with `Failed to load client buffer integration:
   "wayland-egl"` when `/tmp/eglw2` was added to its `LD_LIBRARY_PATH`; using the
   original `LP_PLASMA` without that path made it stay up.
